Tess Bakharia for Hyper Map

Wake up. Power up. Log on.

Open your eyes, heart and mind to your digital reality.

Are you in control: of your appearance, your being, your destiny?

I am here to remind you that digital sovereignty is a myth. The internet, once a place of boundless potential and dreams of digital collectivism, now purged of utopic intentions by technocratic algorithmic rule.

It appears we are not in fact the creators of our own destiny after all, merely a cog in the metaphorical media machine. The NPCs of our MMORPG, resigned to our digital serfdom. Confined each day to the capital system and each night to the realm of digital fiefdoms. Where the waged work ends, the digital labour begins.

It is here you commence your quest.

In this life, among other things, you hope to one day fulfil a greater purpose. Is this what you're seeking as you navigate countless online worlds—a path to self-discovery? To fulfil the deepest parts of your psyche? Digital self-expression and autonomy?

You return day by day, relentlessly searching, filtering, sorting, cataloguing, filing, saving, repeating. Driven by nostalgia for a better time, compelled by a higher power and moved by an incessant need to do something.

Desperate in your search, you transmit a beam of light, an exercise in digital manifestation. 'Are you there?', you motion through carefully curated images, videos and media in the hope that someone or something will signal back.

Along the way, you generate an endless stream of instant gratification through screen-stacking and content grazing. Accruing digital assets and digital waste, expanding the cloud while expanding your soul. You search far and wide, seeking to find the limits of this liminal world, unable to conceptualise its physical footprint.

Every hero has a weakness and yours, my friend, is the system. A weakness intended by design.

In this digital world, everything is scannable, trackable, traceable. In gaining entry, you became complicit, relinquishing your autonomy to the techno-surveillance machine. Just as you search, catalogue, and compile, you are also being mapped. Your genomic sequence, your biometrics, every piece of data you generate is extracted to be leveraged and sold. Even when offline, your digital presence remains.

Is this what you were seeking to find?

Will this data reveal your essence?

Can a computer map your being?

Tell me about yourself—the first time you materialised online, who you work for and who owns you, who you are when not commodified.

What makes you come alive?

Tell me about your life.
Tell me about your thoughts.
Tell me about your dreams.

Now tell me, have you been successful in your quest?

If your goal was to find escapism, did you find it, and how then does it feel to be set free? Do you now know what it feels like to experience everything at the same time, how it feels to be one with the machine?

It is here that the soul and spirit begin.
